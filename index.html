What we're building

Made for Apple devices.

The project is being built with iOS and iPadOS in mind, with Metal and Wii U GamePad controls planned as part of the emulator.

01

Metal

Metal is the main graphics backend we're building around.

02

GamePad

Touch controls are being made around the Wii U GamePad layout.

03

ARM64

The emulator is being developed for the ARM64 hardware used by modern Apple devices.

Status

Still in development.

There isn't a public release yet. We're working on the emulator core, graphics, controls, and the rest of the Wii U system.
